# Getting started in 5 minutes

This page takes you from nothing to your first chat. You do not need to know how to code.

> **Pictures:** lines that start with "Picture:" describe a screenshot that will be added later. They only say what you should see.

## What is a bot?

A **bot** is a helper that works for you on your computer. You write to it like you write to a friend. It can read files, look things up on the web and do small jobs. Before it changes anything, it asks you.

Your first bot is called **Praxi**. You can make more bots later. Each bot can have its own rules.

## Step 1: Install

1. Download the Windows installer (a file that ends in `.exe`) from the project's releases page.
2. Open it. You do not need administrator rights.
3. Windows may show a blue warning ("SmartScreen"). That is normal for new apps. Press **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
4. Praxi opens by itself when the install is done.

More details: [Installing on Windows](install-windows.md).

> **Picture:** the Windows warning with the **More info** and **Run anyway** buttons.

## Step 2: Get a free key

Your bot needs a "brain". That is an AI model from an online service. The service gives you a **key**. A key is a long line of letters and numbers. It works like a password for your bot.

Google Gemini has a free plan. You only need a Google account.

1. On the **Choose a brain** screen, find **Use a free model**.
2. Press **Get my key**. A Google page opens in your browser.
3. Sign in with Google and press **Create API key**. Copy the key.
4. Go back to Praxi and paste the key into the box. Praxi checks it right away.

Your key is saved in your computer's own password store. Praxi never shows it again.

Not ready? Press **I will add this later**. You can look around. Your bot starts answering as soon as you add a key.

More about models and keys: [Providers and models](providers-and-models.md).

> **Picture:** the **Choose a brain** screen with **Use a free model** and the **Get my key** button.

## Step 3: Choose how much freedom

On the **How much freedom?** screen you pick one of three levels:

- **Ask**: the bot checks with you before it changes anything.
- **Balanced** (recommended): the bot looks around on its own and asks before it changes or sends anything.
- **Free**: the bot gets on with it. It still stops for payments.

If you are not sure, keep **Balanced**. You can change it later. See [Keep your bot safe](keep-your-bot-safe.md).

## Step 4: Meet your bot

On the **Meet your bot** screen, give your bot a name and a look. Then press **Start**. Under **Try this first** you see ready-made questions you can click.

> **Picture:** the **Meet your bot** screen with the **Try this first** suggestions.

## Your first chat

1. Click one of the **Try this first** questions, or type your own in the message box.
2. Press **Send**.
3. If the bot wants to do something that needs your OK, a card titled **Approval needed** appears. Read it. Press **Allow once** or **Deny**.
4. To stop the bot at any time, press **Stop**.

Good first questions:

- "Summarise this folder"
- "Help me write a short, friendly message to a neighbour"
- "Find today's top AI news"

> **Picture:** a chat with one **Approval needed** card showing **Allow once** and **Deny**.
