# Quick answers

Short answers to the questions people ask first. For the longer technical questions, see the [FAQ](faq.md).

**What is a bot?**
A helper that works for you on your computer. It asks before it changes anything. See [Getting started in 5 minutes](getting-started.md).

**What does it cost?**
Praxi itself has no price. The AI service may charge. Google Gemini has a free plan. You can set a spending limit for each bot. See [Bot spending limits](bot-spending-limits.md).

**Do I need a key?**
Yes, unless you use a model that runs on your own computer. A key is a long line of letters and numbers from the AI service. It works like a password for your bot.

**Is my data private?**
Your chats and settings stay on your computer. Praxi has no telemetry. What the bot reads for a task goes to the AI service you chose. See [Keep your bot safe](keep-your-bot-safe.md).

**Will the bot do things without asking me?**
On **Balanced** it reads and looks around on its own. It asks before it deletes, sends or spends. You can pick **Careful** to be asked more often.

**How do I stop a bot right now?**
Press **Stop** in the chat. The floating control also has **Stop**.

**Can a bot delete my files?**
Only if you allow it. On **Careful** and **Balanced** it asks first. Read the **Approval needed** card before you press **Allow once**.

**Do I need a server?**
No. Your bots run on your computer. A server is only for jobs that must run while your computer is off. See [Run it on this computer or on a server](local-or-server.md).

**How do I connect Gmail or Calendar?**
Open **Apps**, find the app and press **Connect**. See [Connect your apps](connect-your-apps.md).

**How do I make a bot do something every day?**
Open **Scheduled tasks** and press **New schedule**. See [Automate things](automate.md).

**Does the bot remember me?**
It can keep useful facts, such as your name or how you like things done. They stay on your computer. **Settings → AI and memory** shows what bots remember. See [Bots and profiles](bots-and-profiles.md).

**Something does not work.**
See [Fix common problems](fix-common-problems.md).

**How do I change the language or the look?**
Open **Settings** from the profile menu. **General** has the language. **Appearance** has the theme.
